Recruitment FAQ

What is the typical visa processing time?
For Qatar, the standard visa processing time takes between 15 to 45 business days. This includes document attestation, medical reports, and labor department approvals.
What is your candidate replacement policy?
We offer a 90-day performance guarantee. If a candidate fails to meet standards, we provide a replacement at no additional recruitment cost.
Do you provide post-deployment support?
Yes, ZOD Manpower provides support including grievance handling and orientation follow-ups.
Are all candidates pre-screened?
Absolutely. Every candidate has undergone a rigorous 3-stage screening: Technical, Physical, and Security verification.
